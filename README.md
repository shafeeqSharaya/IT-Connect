# ITConnect

ITConnect is a mobile platform designed to connect IT students and fresh graduates with companies offering internships, jobs, short-term tasks, and skill-building opportunities.

The platform helps students discover opportunities that match their skills and experience while allowing companies to publish opportunities and manage applications through a single platform.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

ITConnect was developed as a university graduation project at Applied Science Private University.

The main goal of the project is to create a platform that reduces the gap between IT students and companies by providing a structured environment for discovering opportunities, applying for tasks or internships, and connecting with potential employers.

The platform supports two main user roles:

* 👨‍🎓 Students / Graduates
* 🏢 Companies

---

## ❗ Problem Statement

Students and fresh graduates often struggle to find suitable opportunities that match their skills, education level, and experience.

At the same time, small and medium-sized companies may need IT professionals for short-term tasks without going through a lengthy recruitment process.

Traditional job platforms are not always designed for short-term technical tasks or opportunities specifically targeting students and fresh graduates.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

ITConnect provides a centralized platform where:

### Students can:

* Create and manage their profiles
* Add technical skills and certificates
* Upload their CV
* Search for opportunities
* Filter opportunities
* Apply for jobs, internships, and short-term tasks
* Track their applications
* Receive notifications

### Companies can:

* Create company profiles
* Publish opportunities
* Define required skills
* Manage posted opportunities
* Review applicants
* Manage applications
* Evaluate candidates

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of ITConnect are:

* Connect IT students and graduates with companies
* Provide access to short-term paid technical tasks
* Help students gain practical experience
* Give companies access to emerging IT talent
* Simplify the recruitment and application process

## ⚙️ Main Features

### 👤 Account Management

* User registration and login
* Role-based accounts
* Authentication
* Profile management

### 👨‍🎓 Student Profile

* Personal information
* Education
* Technical skills
* Certificates
* CV upload
* Experience

### 🏢 Company Profile

* Company information
* Company description
* Published opportunities
* Application management

### 💼 Opportunities

Companies can publish different types of opportunities:

* Short-term tasks
* Internships
* Jobs
* Training opportunities

Tasks can be designed for short periods, allowing students to gain experience while working on real-world projects.

### 🔎 Search & Filtering

Students can discover opportunities using filters such as:

* Skills
* Opportunity type
* Experience level
* Required qualifications

### 📝 Applications

Students can:

* Apply for opportunities
* View application status
* Track submitted applications

Companies can:

* View applicants
* Review candidate information
* Manage applications

### 🔔 Notifications

The system provides notifications for important events such as:

* New opportunities
* Application updates
* Company responses
* Application status changes

## 🧠 AI Matching

One of the planned features of ITConnect is an AI-based matching system.

The system can analyze:

* Student skills
* Education
* Certificates
* Experience

and compare them with:

* Required skills
* Opportunity requirements
* Experience level

This can help students discover opportunities that are relevant to their profiles.
